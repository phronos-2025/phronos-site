## The INS-001 Instrument Family: Semantic Cartography

INS-001 measures two dimensions of cognition:

**Divergence** quantifies how far your semantic associations venture from statistically predictable paths. Higher divergence indicates unexpected conceptual connections.

**Convergence** quantifies how effectively your associations communicate meaning to others—specifically, whether an AI system can reconstruct your target word from your clues. Higher convergence indicates more effective encoding of semantic information.

During the assessment, you provide a target word and five one-word association clues. These inputs are processed to generate your scores. The instrument takes approximately five minutes.
